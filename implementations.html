Other & earlier implementations

http4k-testing/servirtium (David Denton) is an independent, mature Servirtium implementation for Kotlin and Java, shipped as part of the http4k toolkit — demo: servirtium-demo-kotlin-climate-tck (Kotlin and Java/JUnit5), and jbehave-servirtium-climate-tck-demo (JBehave).

Servirtium also began life as standalone per-language libraries, most now folded into servirtium-vcr. They remain in the servirtium GitHub org, with thanks to the people who built them:
